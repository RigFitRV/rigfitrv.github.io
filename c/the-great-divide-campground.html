Located in rural Sussex County in Newton, New Jersey, The Great Divide Campground is a high-energy, family-friendly private campground known for themed weekend events and live concerts. However, navigating the local access roads and tackling internal hill grades with a motorhome or towed vehicle requires specific logistical awareness.

Here is our verified ground-level breakdown to help you navigate navigation approaches, toad unhooking, terrain incline challenges, and site selection.

🚚 The Quick Rig-Fit Scorecard

  • Recommended Max Rig Length: Up to 45 ft (Large Class A sites available)
  • Toad / Towed Vehicle Friendliness: High (Dedicated staging lot just past the entrance gate)
  • Grade / Terrain Warning: Steep hill climb required to access campsite loops
  • Best Big-Rig Sites: Sites G20 through G25 (Recommended for large Class A coaches & motorhomes)
  • Atmosphere: Very family-friendly and event-focused (not ideal for quiet, secluded trips)

🛣️ Navigation & Approach Route

Standard GPS navigation can take larger rigs down tight local cuts. The verified approach route for motorhomes and towed rigs is:

  • From US-206, take Springdale Greendell Rd.
  • Turn onto Huntsville Rd, which transitions directly into Phillips Rd leading into the campground entrance.
⚠️ Steep Hill Grade Warning: Accessing all campsite loops inside the park requires climbing a noticeably steep hill after passing the main entrance. Ensure your transmission, tow hitch, and brakes are ready before making the climb, especially if towing heavy.

🚗 Toad Unhook Strategy

Do not attempt to pull your towed vehicle up the internal hill or through the loop turns while unhooked:

  • Drop your toad at the main office parking area located just past the entrance gate.
  • This area provides ample room and staging space to safely disconnect your tow bar or dolly before attempting the steep internal hill climb and backing into your site pad.

🏕️ Campground Overview & Best Sites

Large Rig Recommendations (Class A & Big Class C)

If you are driving a large Class A coach or a long Class C rig, target Sites G20 through G25. These sites provide superior pad length, better backup geometry, and cleaner overhead clearance compared to tighter wooded back-in loops.

Atmosphere & Weekend Noise Expectation

The Great Divide is famous for its vibrant community atmosphere, hosting weekend concert events and rotating weekly themes. While it is exceptional for families and kids, travelers seeking solitude or quiet deep-woods relaxation should plan accordingly, as weekend activity remains lively throughout the day and evening.
