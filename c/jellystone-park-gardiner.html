🗺️ Approach & Routing Guidance

I-87 Exit 18 to Route 32 South & Bevier Road

Accessed via I-87 (NY State Thruway) Exit 18, heading south on Route 32 (S. Manheim Blvd) to Route 44/55 West (right turn), then turning left onto Albany Post Road South, and left onto Bevier Road. This route provides wide, state-maintained turning paths suitable for large Class A motorhomes and heavy fifth wheels.

🏕️ Campground Overview & Atmosphere

Jellystone Park Gardiner serves as an elite Hudson Valley basecamp offering full 50-amp hookups, level gravel pads, water park attractions, and stunning views of the Shawangunk Ridge.

Red Carpet pull-through rows accommodate large motorhomes up to 60 ft with multiple slide-outs while leaving ample room for towed vehicles. Shaded back-in loops provide natural privacy closer to the Wallkill River corridor.
