🛣️ Regional Approach & Big-Rig Highway Corridors

Navigating the Hershey, Harrisburg, and Brandywine Valley region requires balancing heavy tourist arterial corridors with historic, narrow township lanes. Major access routes such as I-83, US-322, PA-283, and the PA Turnpike (I-76) provide wide lanes and high-clearance bridges for long fifth wheels and Class A motorhomes. Key state connectors like PA-741 and Sweet Street offer straightforward transit to top resort properties without confronting low-clearance residential overpasses.
