RV Water & Plumbing Management Guide

A technical deep dive into RV fresh water systems: water pressure regulation, PEX plumbing limits, filtration setups, 12V pump diagnostics, and backflow prevention at campground spigots.

💧 Water Pressure Regulation: Protecting PEX Fittings & Vinyl Hose

Campground municipal water pressure varies dramatically across North America. While standard residential plumbing operates at 60–80 PSI, standard RV flexible PEX tubing and plastic crimp fittings are rated for a safe continuous working limit of 40 to 50 PSI.

Component / System Safe Operating Range Failure / Damage Risk
Factory RV PEX & Fittings 40 – 50 PSI Splitting soft lines, popping plastic crimp connectors
Onboard Tankless Water Heaters 45 – 55 PSI (Requires min. 0.9 GPM flow) Flow-switch chatter or cold-water sandwiching under low pressure
Unregulated Campground Spigots 60 – 110+ PSI (Varies by location) Catastrophic internal flooding, bursting freshwater drinking hoses

Adjustable Brass Regulators vs. Fixed Restrictors

Cheap fixed-rate brass restrictors narrow the water orifice but do not maintain static pressure regulation. When faucets are closed, static line pressure equalizes with the campground spigot, subjecting your internal lines to full pressure surges. Always deploy an adjustable brass pressure regulator with an oil-filled pressure gauge directly at the campground spigot before attaching your hose.

Pro Plumbing Tip: Always attach your pressure regulator to the campground spigot first, before connecting your drinking hose. This protects both your drinking hose and internal plumbing from high-pressure spikes.

🛡️ Water Filtration & Contaminant Protection

Campground water sources range from chlorinated municipal supplies to untreated deep well water containing high mineral concentrations, sediment, and sulfur.

  • Stage 1 (Sediment Filtration): 5-micron inline spun polypropylene filters remove rust particles, sand, and visible debris before water enters your lines.
  • Stage 2 (Activated Carbon Block): Eliminates chlorine taste, odor, heavy metals, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs).
  • Dual-Canister Exterior Setups: Mount external dual-stage canisters on your utility bay bumper or ground frame for high-flow filtration without sacrificing interior water pressure.

🚰 City Water Connection vs. Fresh Tank Gravity Fill

Understanding when to run off municipal pressure versus your onboard 12V demand pump prevents check-valve failure and internal freshwater tank overflow.

  • City Water Inlet: Feeds interior fixtures directly using external spigot pressure. Contains an internal check valve to prevent city water from back-flowing into your fresh holding tank. If your fresh tank fills on its own while hooked to city water, this internal check valve has failed or seated improperly.
  • Fresh Water Tank & 12V Pump: Fill the holding tank via gravity or city fill selector valve. Turn off the 12V water pump when connected to city pressure to avoid pump cycling and motor wear.
