🏖️ Why Ocean City & Assateague is an Elite Coastal RV Destination

The Ocean City and Assateague Island corridor combines coastal barrier island scenery, bayfront water access, and high-end resort amenities. Whether you're setting up a 45-foot Class A motorhome along Sinepuxent Bay, camping among wild horses at Assateague State Park, or staging a travel trailer in shaded woodland loops near Berlin and Newark, this cluster provides diverse camping environments within minutes of the Atlantic coast.

🚚 Rig Clearance & Heavy Highway Routing Guidance

Reaching Maryland's coastal RV resorts requires navigating major east-west corridors and low-country bridges. Summer weekend traffic creates significant bottlenecks near the Chesapeake Bay Bridge (US-50/301) and the Route 50/113 split in Berlin.

Key Highway Approaches

  • US-50 East (Ocean Gateway): Primary four-lane dualized highway into Ocean City. Paved and wide, but heavy summer traffic slows transit through Salisbury and Easton. Ensure high-profile rigs maintain safe follow distances during abrupt stop-and-go conditions.
  • US-113 South (Worcester Highway): Ideal access for parks located in Berlin, Newark, and Whaleyville (including Fort Whaley and Island Resort). Smooth multi-lane layout bypassing central Ocean City strip traffic.
  • MD-611 South (Stephen Decatur Hwy): Dedicated corridor heading south onto Sinepuxent Neck toward Sun Outdoors Frontier Town and Assateague State Park. Flat, well-paved road, but watch for cyclists and slow-moving beach traffic near the Verrazano Bridge crossing.
Pro Clearance Tip: Crossing the Verrazano Bridge into Assateague Island presents strong crosswinds along Sinepuxent Bay. High-profile fifth wheels and travel trailers should secure awnings and monitor sway control. When camping on Assateague Island, maintain strict distance from wild horses—they will investigate unlatched outdoor kitchens and storage bays.

⚡ Local Infrastructure & Utility Insights

  • Power Grid Loads: Extreme summer temperatures along the coastal plain drive heavy dual-A/C usage across large resorts. Voltage drops below 108V can occur during peak mid-afternoon hours; an EMS/surge protector with low-voltage cutoff is strongly recommended.
  • Water Pressure & Salt Air Corrosion: Municipal and well water systems throughout Worcester County exhibit variable pressure. Use an adjustable water pressure regulator. Coastal salt spray accelerates corrosion on exposed hitch components and slide-out hardware—apply silicone lubricant to rubber seals.
  • Cellular & Wi-Fi Coverage: Excellent 5G coverage across Ocean City and Berlin on Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile. Coverage drops slightly inside dense pine loops at state parks.
