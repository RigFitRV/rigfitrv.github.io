Arterial Highway Routing: Garden State Parkway & US-9

The primary spine for South Jersey coastal transit is the Garden State Parkway (GSP) and US Route 9. Rigs traveling south should remain on state/county highways and avoid cutting through tight residential beach streets, where overhanging oak/pine branches and low utility lines present severe roof hazards.

  • GSP Exit 4 & Exit 10: Primary exits for Cape May and Cape May Court House resort properties (Beachcomber, Sun Outdoors Cape May, Sun Retreats Seashore).
  • GSP Exit 13 & Exit 17: Key access corridors for Sea Isle City and Ocean View destinations (Sun Retreats Avalon, Ocean View Resort, Pine Haven).
  • Pinelands Access (CR-542): Rigs heading into Egg Harbor City or Batsto (Belhaven Lake) should utilize CR-542 from GSP Exit 50/52, maintaining center positioning on rural pine corridors.
